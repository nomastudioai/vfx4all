How it works

  1. 01Pick an effect

    Each preset is an open JSON file: a film scene, an effect and its sound. Copy one to make your own.

  2. 02Add a photo or a clip

    Qwen Image Edit 2511 builds the first frame with your subject's face, or the effect starts from the frame you choose in your video.

  3. 03LTX-2.5 renders it

    Open-weights LTX-2.5 animates the shot with native audio on Comfy Cloud or on your own GPU.
