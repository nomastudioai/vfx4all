# Samples

Files to try VFX 4 All without your own material. They show members of the NoMa Studio AI team who
agreed to this use, so they are **not** covered by the MIT license: use them only to test VFX 4 All,
and do not reuse them elsewhere.

| File | What it is | Try it with |
|---|---|---|
| `photos/front.jpg` and `photos/side.jpg` | Two photos of the same person, a front view and a side view, plain background | Photo mode, both photos at once, subject "Man" |
| `clips/street-walk-16x9.mp4` | 6 s, 896x512, a man walking toward the camera on a city avenue at sunrise, with sound. Generated with VFX 4 All's own LTX-2.5 graph from a photo scene, to show that AI clips work too | Video mode, cut at about 1.5 s, Walk of Fire, Disintegrate or Portal Jump |
| `clips/rooftop-walk-vertical.mp4` | 3.4 s, 720x1280, a woman walking toward a phone camera on a rooftop, no sound. Real phone footage | Video mode, cut at about 1 s, subject "Woman", Shockwave or Lightning Strike |

The photos have their metadata removed.
